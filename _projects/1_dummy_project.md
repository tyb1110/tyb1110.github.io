Every good portfolio needs at least one slightly ridiculous project, right? Here's mine.

## The Idea

I decided to train a model to predict whether I'd like a coffee based on various features:

- Roast darkness (1-10 scale)
- Caffeine content (mg)
- Price (because fancy = better? Not always!)
- Whether it's a Monday
- How many hours of sleep I got

## The Reality

Turns out, my coffee preferences are more chaotic than a random number generator. The model achieved a stunning **52% accuracy** — practically groundbreaking! 🎉

## What I Learned

1. Humans are irrational (or at least, I am)
2. Not every problem needs machine learning
3. Sometimes the best model is just... asking me directly
4. Training data matters, but so does real-world variability
