A logical fallacy is a flaw in reasoning — the conclusion doesn't actually follow from the premises, even when the argument sounds persuasive. This dossier sorts 22 common fallacies into four families (formal, relevance, presumption, ambiguity), gives two worked examples for each, and includes a 27-question quiz for testing recognition in a workshop or classroom setting.

Tap any card below to reveal its examples. Use the tabs to browse by family, or jump into Quiz Mode.
